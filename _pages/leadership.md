# Leadership & Impact

Beyond research, I am actively engaged in building institutions, shaping policy discourse, and translating AI research into real-world impact. This page consolidates my leadership roles, research funding, honors, media presence, and professional services.

---

## Academic Leadership

<div class='paper-box'>
  <div class='paper-box-text' markdown="1">

**Head of the Large Language Model Division**  
[Hong Kong Generative AI Research and Development Centre (HKGAI)](https://www.hkgai.info/)  
*2024 – present*  
As a founding member of HKGAI and lead author of the HKGAI-V1 technical report, I lead the LLM Division, overseeing the development of Hong Kong's sovereign large language model. This role combines direct engineering access to a sovereign LLM with strategic leadership in AI governance.

  </div>
</div>

[HKUST-DXM AI for Finance Joint Laboratory](https://hkust.edu.hk/)  
*January 2024 – present*  
Joint appointment across the Division of Emerging Interdisciplinary Areas, Department of Economics, and Division of Arts and Machine Creativity at HKUST.
